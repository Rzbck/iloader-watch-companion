# Code of conduct

This project welcomes technical discussion, testing, documentation, and code-review feedback from anyone acting in good faith.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and specific when discussing code, tests, or failures.
- Critique implementations and evidence, not people.
- Clearly distinguish confirmed behavior from hypotheses.
- Respect privacy: do not repost credentials, device identifiers, private logs, or personal data.
- Give credit for upstream and community work.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, deliberate disclosure of private information, or intentionally misleading security reports are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Repository maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove contributions that violate these expectations and may restrict participation when necessary to protect contributors or users.

For sensitive security matters, follow `SECURITY.md` instead of using a public discussion.
